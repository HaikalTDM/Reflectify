# Privacy Policy for Reflectify

**Last Updated:** October 21, 2025

## Introduction

Welcome to Reflectify ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience while using our mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Reflectify.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you create an account:

- **Email Address**: Used for account creation, authentication, and password recovery
- **Password**: Encrypted and stored securely for account access

### 2. Usage Data

We automatically collect certain information about your use of the app:

- **Reflection Statistics**: Number of reflections completed, daily streaks, quiz scores
- **Bookmarks**: Hadiths you save for future reference
- **App Usage**: Features you use and how you interact with the app
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, and app version

### 3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:
- Location data
- Contacts
- Photos or media files
- Personal identification documents
- Browsing history outside the app

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

1. **Account Management**
   - Create and manage your user account
   - Authenticate your identity
   - Enable password recovery

2. **App Functionality**
   - Sync your data across multiple devices
   - Track your personal progress and streaks
   - Save your bookmarked hadiths
   - Customize your experience

3. **App Improvement**
   - Analyze usage patterns to improve features
   - Fix bugs and technical issues
   - Develop new features based on user needs

4. **Notifications**
   - Send daily reminders for reflections (if enabled)
   - Notify you about app updates

## Data Storage and Security

### Cloud Storage
We use **Supabase** (a secure, open-source database platform) to store:
- Your account information
- Reflection statistics
- Bookmarks
- User preferences

### Security Measures
- All data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS
- Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
- Supabase implements industry-standard security practices
- We regularly update our security measures

### Data Retention
- Your data is retained as long as your account is active
- You can request data deletion at any time (see Your Rights section)
- Deleted data is permanently removed within 30 days

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

### 1. Supabase (Database & Authentication)
- **Purpose**: Store user data and handle authentication
- **Data Shared**: Email, encrypted password, usage statistics, bookmarks
- **Privacy Policy**: https://supabase.com/privacy
- **Location**: Data stored in secure cloud servers

### 2. DeepSeek AI
- **Purpose**: Generate contextual quiz questions based on hadiths
- **Data Shared**: Hadith text only (no personal information)
- **Privacy Policy**: https://www.deepseek.com/privacy
- **Note**: No personal user data is sent to DeepSeek

### 3. Hadith API
- **Purpose**: Fetch authentic hadith texts and translations
- **Data Shared**: None (read-only API access)
- **Note**: No personal data is transmitted

### 4. Toyyibpay (Payment Processing)
- **Purpose**: Process voluntary donations
- **Data Shared**: Donation amount only
- **Privacy Policy**: https://toyyibpay.com/privacy-policy
- **Note**: Payment details are handled entirely by Toyyibpay; we do not store or access your payment information

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT:
- Sell your personal information
- Share your data with advertisers
- Use your data for marketing purposes
- Share your data with third parties except as described in this policy

We may disclose your information only in the following circumstances:
- **Legal Requirements**: If required by law or legal process
- **Safety**: To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users
- **Business Transfer**: In the event of a merger or acquisition (users will be notified)

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

### 1. Access Your Data
- View your profile information within the app
- Export your bookmarks and statistics (contact support)

### 2. Update Your Data
- Update your email address in app settings
- Change your password anytime

### 3. Delete Your Data
- Delete your account and all associated data through app settings
- Contact us at [your-support-email@example.com] to request complete data deletion
- We will delete your data within 30 days of your request

### 4. Opt-Out of Notifications
- Disable push notifications in app settings or device settings
- You'll still be able to use all app features

### 5. Data Portability
- Request a copy of your data in machine-readable format
- Contact support for data export requests

## Children's Privacy

Reflectify is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to delete such information.

## Cookies and Tracking

## International Data Transfers
